# compliance-conditioned-molgen

Research track 25. Two questions share this repository.

1. How many entries of the ECHA Candidate List, REACH Annex XIV and REACH Annex XVII can be written as one machine-readable structure?
2. Can a small SELFIES language model be steered toward a UV-Vis absorption band while passing a structural-pattern screen, compared with an identical unsteered model?

Safety scope: controlled substances and drug precursors are a blocklist only. They are never a conditioning target and never a reward. The repository contains no synthesis routes, no retrosynthesis and no hazardous or controlled structures.

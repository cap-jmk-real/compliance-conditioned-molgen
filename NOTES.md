# Where this came from

This repository holds research track #25, compliance-conditioned molecular generation, from
Julian Kleber's research inventory in `cap-jmk-real/open-research`.

- The literature review and research plan live in open-research at
  `docs/25-compliance-conditioned-molecular-generation.md`.
- The data pipeline and coverage measurement in this repo were first merged into open-research as
  PR #4 (squash commit `c5d3c6dea0f3d7706cd3deda274db50cd823bf7d`, 2026-11-16) and moved here so the
  track has its own repository. open-research keeps a pointer only.

## Safety rule

Controlled substances and drug precursors are used as a blocklist only. They are never a
conditioning target or a reward. No synthesis routes, no retrosynthesis, and no hazardous or
controlled structures are committed here.

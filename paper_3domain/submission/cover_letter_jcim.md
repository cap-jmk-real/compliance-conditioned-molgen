Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript entitled *“Compliance-Conditioned Molecular Generation Across Optical,
Polymer, and Central Nervous System Targets with Graph Neural Network Ensembles”* for consideration
as an Article in the *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling*.

The work introduces a single, reproducible pipeline that couples hard regulatory and safety gates
with reinforcement-learning steering against graph-neural-network property ensembles, audited by
independent fingerprint holdout predictors that never enter the training reward. We demonstrate the
framework on three chemically distinct targets—visible absorption engineering, high-temperature
polymer glass transition, and non-hallucinogenic serotonergic ligand design—using only public datasets
and open-source tooling.

**ChemRxiv preprint disclosure.** A related version of this manuscript was posted on ChemRxiv on
19 November 2026 (DOI to be inserted upon posting) to solicit early feedback on the unified
three-domain evaluation. The present submission expands venue-specific reporting (data availability,
supplementary material structure, citation breadth) and wires every headline metric to archived
result files in the public Git repository. No clinical or experimental data are included.

We confirm that the manuscript is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and that all authors
have approved submission. The corresponding author will maintain the code repository accompanying
the paper.
